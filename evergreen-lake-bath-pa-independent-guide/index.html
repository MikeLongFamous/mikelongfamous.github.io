02 / Camping

Camping facts the public page actually states.

Public-source facts · reviewed September 20, 2026

  • Site types: RV, camper, and tent camping; the page says sites are wooded and shady.
  • At each site: a picnic table and fire ring are publicly stated.
  • Connections: the page lists electric, water, internet, and sewer among available amenities; availability is not stated for every site.
  • Rentals: daily, weekend, weekly, monthly, and seasonal terms are offered.
  • Deposits: one night is required to reserve; the page says deposits are non-refundable and holiday weekends must be paid in full and are non-refundable.
  • Unit size: the page says units over 35 feet incur an extra charge; 50-amp details require a call.

PFBC context

Why “license-free” can be lawful

PFBC says patrons of a licensed Class A regulated fishing lake may catch, kill, and possess fish without the normal size, season, or possession limits and do not need a Pennsylvania fishing license.

Independent commentary: the owner’s posted terms resemble Class A fee-fishing rules, but this guide did not independently verify Evergreen Lake’s current PFBC license status. Ask the operator or PFBC if confirmation matters to your visit.

06 / Before you go

Seven things to verify directly.

  1. 01
    Your exact date and site availability.

    Especially for weekends, holidays, or monthly/seasonal stays.

  2. 02
    Site fit and utility needs.

    Share your full rig length and ask about 50 amp, sewer, leveling, and the over-35-foot charge.

  3. 03
    Deposit and cancellation consequences.

    The public page says deposits are non-refundable; ask about rescheduling, no-shows, and early departure.

  4. 04
    Current payment methods.

    The public camping and fishing pages do not clearly state every accepted method.

  5. 05
    Pet access by activity.

    The fishing page says no pets. Ask separately about campground pet rules and where pets may go.

  6. 06
    Facility condition and accessibility.

    Ask whether the specific bathhouse, beach, arcade, trail, or activity you need is open and suitable.

  7. 07
    Event date and weather plan.

    The official schedule may change, and the October source row is ambiguous.

Independent commentary: this checklist is practical advice derived from gaps or time-sensitive items in the public pages. It is not a statement that a listed problem exists.
